# Plan: Explain Home and Report Numbers

## Goal

Help people understand what the numbers mean and where they came from.

## What to change

1. For each number, show a short explanation and the dates it covers.
2. On Home, add a **How was this worked out?** link. It should show the simple steps used to get the number and the payments included in it.
3. In Reports, show the same explanation for each saved report. Use the dates and numbers saved with that report, so older reports still make sense later.
4. Make it easier to look through reports. Show one report at a time, and keep extra details closed until someone chooses to open them.
5. Clearly point out items that were left out, such as payments planned for future months.

## How to tell it works

- People can see what each number means and which dates it covers.
- People can open the details and see how the number was worked out and which payments were counted.
- Anything left out is explained in plain language.
- Opening details does not change anything in YNAB.
- If an older report no longer has all its payment details, the report still opens and explains what details are missing.
